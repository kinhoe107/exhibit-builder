Depending on the files and options selected, the Software may:

a. place printed page numbers or other selected information onto generated PDF pages;
b. scale or render pages to the selected output size;
c. render supported Word documents, spreadsheets, emails and other files into PDF form;
d. invoke locally installed software, including Microsoft Excel where required or available, to improve spreadsheet rendering fidelity;
e. perform optical character recognition or text extraction;
f. create local temporary files, previews, recovery information, preferences and licence-acceptance records; and
g. preserve local project information so that interrupted work may be recovered.

The Software is designed not to alter the user's original source documents. Generated bundles, previews, temporary copies and project files are separate outputs.
